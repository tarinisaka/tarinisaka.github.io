Paper Accepted!

Our paper, When LLM Governance Falls to Employees: Informal Practices of Responsible AI Use at Work, has been accepted to AIES 2026! The conference received over 1,000 submissions, with 220 papers accepted following peer review.

22 June 2026

Presented at the Cambridge Cybercrime Conference 2026

I presented my paper, AI-Enhanced Email Security: A Novel Pipeline for Phishing Campaign Detection and Profiling, and discussed my broader PhD research at the Cambridge Cybercrime Conference 2026.

The work presents a human-AI pipeline for extracting phishing-email features, detecting coordinated campaigns, and generating campaign-level profiles to support security analysts.
